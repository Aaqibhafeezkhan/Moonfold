# Code of conduct

Moonfold is built in the open by people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. We
want participation to be welcoming and free of harassment for everyone.

## Expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Keep discussions on topic and give others room to speak.
- Credit other people's work, including upstream projects.
- Accept decisions gracefully, and say so plainly when you disagree.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or sexualized language and imagery.
- Discrimination based on any personal characteristic.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spam, vote manipulation, or other attempts to game the project or its metrics.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, discussions, pull requests, and any community channel)
and when you represent the project elsewhere. Report problems privately to the maintainers through
GitHub's report feature or by opening a private security advisory. Maintainers may edit or remove
content and may warn, suspend or ban participants who break these rules. Reports are handled
confidentially.

This code of conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
